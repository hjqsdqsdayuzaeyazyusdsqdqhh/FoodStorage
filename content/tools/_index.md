Tools are the fastest way to use this site. Instead of reading a full guide when you need one number, use the shelf-life chart for durations, the storage guides for methods, and the printable checklists for action.

## What's here now

- **The food shelf life chart** — every food we cover, with pantry, fridge, and freezer times in one table. This is the tool people use most: [open the chart](/shelf-life/).
- **Step-by-step storage guides** — freezing, refrigerator storage, dry goods, vacuum sealing, and canning, with equipment lists and storage-times tables. [Browse the methods](/storage-methods/).
- **Pantry checklists** — the [pantry inventory checklist](/pantry-organization/pantry-inventory-checklist/) and [seasonal cleanout checklist](/pantry-organization/seasonal-pantry-cleanout/) are printable and designed to live on the pantry door.

## Coming next

Interactive calculators are in development: a shelf-life calculator, an expiration-date checker, and a pantry audit generator. Each will duplex with the guides and shelf-life pages already here. Until they launch, the chart and checklists below give you the same answers.

The rest of this page links to the guides and shelf-life pages that act as the working tools of the site today.
