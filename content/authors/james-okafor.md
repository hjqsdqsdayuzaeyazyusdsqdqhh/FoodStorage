James Okafor is the senior writer at Home Storage Guide. He translates USDA, FDA, and CDC food-safety guidance — plus university extension research — into plain-language shelf-life answers and step-by-step storage methods.

Before joining Home Storage Guide, James spent eight years as a food-safety auditor for commercial kitchens, where he wrote HACCP plans and trained staff on cold-storage and rotation procedures. That experience is why every guide he writes starts with a real number and a real temperature, never a guess.

At Home Storage Guide he covers the three pillars: how long foods last in the pantry, fridge, and freezer; how to store foods so they last longer; and the pantry systems — FIFO, zones, labels, inventory — that make food visible and keep it out of the trash.

His writing goal is simple: give you one answer you can act on in the next thirty seconds, with the citation ready if you want to verify it.
