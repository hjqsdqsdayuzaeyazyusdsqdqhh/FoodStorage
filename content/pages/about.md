Home Storage Guide tells you exactly how long food lasts and how to keep it safe. That sentence is not a marketing tagline — it is the only thing we do.

The average household wastes hundreds of dollars a year on food that was perfectly safe, because nobody could tell how long it actually lasts. Best-by dates are a quality guess, not an expiration date. The jar at the back of the shelf was probably fine. We built this site so you can look up the exact answer in thirty seconds.

## What we cover

Three pillars, nothing else:

1. **Shelf life** — how long hundreds of foods last in the pantry, fridge, and freezer, plus how to tell when they are actually bad.
2. **Storage methods** — freezing, vacuum sealing, canning, and dry-goods storage, step by step.
3. **Pantry organization** — FIFO rotation, zones, labels, and inventory systems that keep food visible and used.

## How we work

Every page lists its author and the credentialed reviewer who checked it, with the review date. Numeric claims — shelf-life durations and temperatures — are cited inline to USDA, FDA, CDC, or a university extension. When we do not know the answer, we say so and link to the source that does.
