We get things wrong occasionally, and when we do we fix it fast, transparently, and permanently.

## How to report an error

Email hello@homestorageguide.com with "correction" in the subject line. Include:

- The page URL.
- The exact text you believe is wrong.
- What you believe it should say, and your source (a link helps).

## What happens next

1. **Verify.** A member of the editorial team checks the claim against the authoritative source. Numbers must match USDA, FDA, CDC, or a university extension.
2. **Fix.** If the report is confirmed, the page is corrected and the Sources section updated.
3. **Re-review.** The corrected page is re-reviewed by Rebecca Torres, RD, and the review date on the page is updated.
4. **Log.** The correction is recorded in our correction log with the date, the page, the error, and the fix.

## When guidance changes

Food-safety guidance changes. When USDA, FDA, or CDC updates a recommendation — a temperature, a storage time, a labeling rule — we update the affected pages within the quarter, mark them as revised, and move their next re-review date up.
