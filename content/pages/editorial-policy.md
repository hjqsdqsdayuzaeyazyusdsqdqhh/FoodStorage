Home Storage Guide publishes food-storage and food-safety guidance. Because the stakes are your health, our editorial rules are strict.

## What we will and will not publish

We publish shelf-life tables, storage methods, and pantry systems backed by tier-1 sources: USDA (including FSIS and the FoodKeeper App), FDA, CDC, and university extensions. We do not publish personal "I kept it for a month and it was fine" advice as safety guidance, and we never present a best-by date as an expiration date.

## Sources first

Every numeric claim — a duration, a temperature, a spoilage test — is tied to a source. Each page ends with a Sources section listing the authorities behind it, and the most safety-critical pages link the relevant source inline. If a number cannot be sourced, it is removed.

## Review process

- Every page names its **author** and **reviewer**, with a **review date**.
- Safety-tier topics — eggs, poultry, ground meat, seafood, cooked rice, low-acid canned foods, canning, and food-safety temperature guides — require sign-off by Rebecca Torres, RD, our credentialed reviewer.
- All pages are re-reviewed on a schedule: every 6 months for safety-tier content, every 12 months for everything else.

## Independence from advertisers

Affiliate and advertising relationships never change what we recommend or how we test. Affiliate links are labeled, use `rel="sponsored"`, and are disclosed in plain text. If we cannot say something honestly and still keep the revenue, we say it honestly.

## Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it fast and publicly. See our [corrections policy](/about/corrections/).

## Who to hold accountable

Editor-in-chief Rebecca Torres, RD, owns this policy and the review process. Reach the team at [our contact page](/contact/).
