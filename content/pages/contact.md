We read everything that comes in. Here is who to write to and what we need from you.

## Corrections

Spotted a number that does not match the source, or a link that is broken? Send us the page URL, the exact text, and what you believe is correct. We verify every correction against the source before we change anything. See our [corrections policy](/about/corrections/) for how we handle it.

## Content requests

Looking for a food or method we have not covered? Tell us the question exactly as you would search it — for example, "how long does almond milk last after opening." Requests help us choose what to write next.

## Advertising and partnerships

We accept clearly labeled advertising and affiliate partnerships that meet our [editorial policy](/about/editorial-policy/). Ads never influence content.

## Editorial team

- **Editor-in-chief:** Rebecca Torres, RD — food-safety review and content standards
- **Senior writer:** James Okafor — shelf-life and storage-method guides

Email us at **hello@homestorageguide.com**. For faster handling, put "correction", "content request", or "partnership" in the subject line. We aim to reply within a few business days.
