## General information only

Everything published on this Site is **general information**, not medical, dietary, or professional advice. Food-safety guidance can prevent illness, and it can change as authorities update their recommendations. Always follow the current guidance of the USDA, FDA, and CDC, and consult a qualified professional — such as a registered dietitian or your doctor — about your specific situation.

## Shelf-life figures can vary

Shelf-life figures on this site are typical ranges based on published guidance from food-safety authorities, and assume proper storage. Actual results vary with temperature, packaging, handling, and the specific product. "Best by" and "use by" dates are quality and safety cues, not guarantees. When in doubt, throw it out: our articles always explain how to tell whether a food has actually gone bad.
