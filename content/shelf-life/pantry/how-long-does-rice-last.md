Rice is the pantry's quiet champion. A bag of white rice can sit for years and cook perfectly — the only things that can end it are moisture, bugs, and time in a warm kitchen. Brown rice is the opposite: the very bran that makes it healthier is what makes it spoil, because its oils turn rancid in months.

The whole rice-storage story is one decision: know your rice. White rice stores long; brown rice must be kept cold or eaten soon.

## How long rice lasts

| Storage method | White rice | Brown rice | Best practice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pantry (airtight) | 2+ years | 3–6 months | Cool, dark, dry; airtight container |
| Pantry (opened bag) | 6–12 months | 1–3 months | Transfer to airtight container |
| Fridge | Not needed | 6–12 months | Airtight container |
| Freezer | Indefinite | Indefinite | Airtight container |

## Storage conditions for rice

Store rice in an airtight container — glass, plastic, or food-grade bucket — in a cool, dark pantry. Airtight matters twice over: it keeps moisture and pantry bugs out, and it keeps the rice from absorbing odors. If you buy rice in bags, transfer it to a container rather than clipping the bag shut.

For brown rice, the pantry is the wrong home. Its bran oils start oxidizing the moment it's milled. Refrigerating or freezing brown rice in an airtight container extends it from months to a year or more. Buy brown rice in amounts you'll use, or freeze the extra.

::tip
Freeze every new bag of rice for a few days before moving it to your pantry container. It kills any pantry-moth eggs that came with the bag — the most common way bugs get into a "clean" pantry.
::

## How to tell rice has gone bad

Three signs to check: bugs, mold, and rancidity. Pantry moths, weevils, or tiny larvae in the container mean the rice is contaminated — discard the bag and clean the container. Mold looks like white or gray fuzz and smells musty. Brown rice goes rancid with an acrid, bitter-nutty smell. If anything smells off or looks infested, throw it out.

::note
Rancid brown rice is not a safety hazard in the way spoiled meat is, but it tastes genuinely bad and the off flavor gets stronger with time. If it smells acrid or bitter, do not cook it — the whole pot will taste wrong.
::

## Long-term rice storage

For emergency or long-term storage, white rice is one of the best foods to stock. Stored in sealed, food-grade containers with oxygen absorbers at cool temperatures, white rice lasts 5–10 years or more. Keep it dry above all — moisture is the only thing that can truly ruin a properly sealed stash.
