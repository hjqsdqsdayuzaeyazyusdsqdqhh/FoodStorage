faq:
  - q: How long does raw meat last in the refrigerator?
    a: With the fridge at 40°F or below, raw poultry and ground meat last 1–2 days, and steaks, chops, and roasts last 3–5 days. Cooked meat lasts 3–4 days. If you will not cook it in that window, freeze it — frozen raw meat stays safe indefinitely and best for 3–12 months.
  - q: How long can meat sit out before it is unsafe?
    a: No more than 2 hours at room temperature — and only 1 hour if the room is above 90°F. Bacteria multiply fastest in the 40–140°F danger zone. Meat left out longer should be thrown away, even if it looks and smells fine, because you cannot see or smell the harmful bacteria.
  - q: Can you refreeze meat after thawing?
    a: Yes, if it was thawed safely in the refrigerator. Raw meat thawed in the fridge can be refrozen within the 1–2 day window, though quality suffers slightly. Never refreeze meat thawed on the counter — refreezing does not kill bacteria. Cooking the meat and freezing the cooked result is always safe.
  - q: What is the danger zone temperature?
    a: The danger zone is 40°F to 140°F, where bacteria double in number as quickly as every 20 minutes. Keep cold food at or below 40°F and hot food at or above 140°F. See the full food safety temperature guide for details.

Meat is the highest-risk food in your kitchen, and the rules are simple enough to memorize in one reading. Cold meat — raw or cooked — stays safe for a specific number of days. Warm meat is never safe for long.

The two facts that matter most: raw poultry and ground meat keep only 1–2 days in the fridge, and any perishable food left between 40°F and 140°F for more than 2 hours should be thrown away. That second rule is the one most people violate — a raw chicken left on the counter for the afternoon looks fine, but the bacteria you cannot see are having a party.

## Raw vs. cooked, and the freezer backup

- **Raw poultry (chicken, turkey):** 1–2 days refrigerated; freeze for 9 months (parts) to 12 months (whole).
- **Raw ground meat:** 1–2 days refrigerated; freeze for 3–4 months.
- **Raw steaks and roasts:** 3–5 days refrigerated; freeze for 6–12 months.
- **Cooked meat and poultry:** 3–4 days refrigerated; freeze for 2–6 months.

If your fridge window is about to expire and you will not cook tonight, freeze. Freezing at 0°F stops bacteria, so frozen meat stays safe indefinitely — the dates above are about quality, not safety.

## Defrosting — the one rule people break

Thaw meat in the refrigerator, in cold water, or in the microwave — never on the counter. A counter-thawed steak spends hours in the danger zone. Refrigerator-thawed meat can be refrozen within the 1–2 day window. For the full process, see [how to freeze meat](/storage-methods/freezing/how-to-freeze-meat/).
